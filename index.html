FAQ

Is the app free?

Yes. You only pay for the boat.

Who assigns my boat?

PBOFA, through a fair weekly rotation. You’re notified once it’s confirmed.

Where do we meet?

Patar White Beach, Bolinao, 15 minutes before your trip.

How do I become an operator?

Sign up as Boat Operator with your Valid ID and Permit to Operate. PBOFA reviews it within 24 hours.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Android only for now.
